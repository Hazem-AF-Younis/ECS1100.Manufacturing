Advanced Manufacturing, Biomanufacturing & Supply Chain Jobs

Real postings checked on LinkedIn in November 2026, ten for each Jonsson School major. Pick your major to see which skills keep coming up, then open any posting to read a summary and the saved PDF.

Tips and notes

  • Every posting was open when captured. Each one was accepting applications on LinkedIn when checked (Nov 1, 2026, or Nov 8 for 14 replacement postings). Listings close over time, so the saved PDF keeps the full text after a job comes down.
  • Read the requirement line. The degree and eligibility rules come from the employer. Some roles name specific majors, and some need U.S. citizenship (often for export-control or ITAR reasons).
  • Each major has 10 postings: 4 internships or co-ops, 3 early-career and 3 experienced. Biomedical Engineering has only 1 early-career posting, because few entry biomanufacturing roles named the major.
  • Experienced roles still help. Read them to see where a career goes in 5 to 8 years and which skills to start building now.
  • Skills are counted once per posting from the posting text, so they show what employers ask for, not a ranking of importance.
Source: LinkedIn job postings captured Nov 1 and Nov 8, 2026 for ECS 1100 (UT Dallas, Jonsson School). Summaries are short excerpts of each posting; the employer's posting governs. Pay is shown only when the posting lists a range.
